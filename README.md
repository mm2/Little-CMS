# LittleCMS PRO

For more than 25 years, I have developed and maintained Little CMS and made it freely available under the MIT license. Behind that code are decades of work on color accuracy, performance, compatibility, and fixes for problems found in real applications.

With the advent of AI, I have seen that work copied and repackaged without acknowledging its origin or contributing back to the project. Producing a copy has become easier. Taking responsibility for its correctness and maintenance still requires expertise and sustained effort.

**LittleCMS PRO will support continued development:** a commercial edition with new capabilities, advanced algorithms, and professional support.

**The community edition remains free under the MIT license** and will continue to receive security updates. Hobbyists, students, researchers, and anyone who needs a free color engine are welcome to keep using it.

If you choose a port or derivative, check how its color accuracy is tested, who maintains it, and whether fixes from the original project reach its users.

Visit [www.littlecms.com](https://www.littlecms.com) for details.

# About Little CMS community

Little CMS community intends to be an **OPEN SOURCE** small-footprint color management engine, with special focus on accuracy and performance. It uses the International Color Consortium standard (ICC), which is the modern standard when regarding to color management. The ICC specification is widely used and is referred to in many International and other de-facto standards. It was approved as an International Standard, ISO 15076-1, in 2005. 


# Conformance
Little CMS is a **FULL IMPLEMENTATION** of ICC specification 4.4, it fully supports all kind of V2 and V4 profiles, including abstract, devicelink and named color profiles. Check the tutorial for a exhaustive list of features. 


# A bit of story
Since the initial release, back in 1998, Little CMS has grown to become one of the most popular open-source color management libraries, and has been used in a large number of production projects, in areas as printer firmware, monitors, digital cameras, RIPs, publishing, scientific, and many others. You can find Little CMS in most Linux distributions, and it's released under an open source license.
